# CampusKart Design System

## Philosophy
A hostel corridor noticeboard brought to life. Dark, warm, low-glare, and tactile.

## Visual Rules
- **The Corkboard**: The main feed uses a masonry grid where cards have a slight, stable rotation (+/- 1.5 degrees).
- **Price Tags**: Every price is rendered inside a clipped-corner tag shape with a punch hole.
- **The Pin**: A small 6px dot (chalk-400) in the corner of cards.
- **Highlighter Accent**: marker-yellow is the ONLY color used for primary actions.
